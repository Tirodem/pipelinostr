- Secret handling: Proxy approach failed (typeof), settled on unwrap-before-init with plain-object-only recursion
- System dependency auto-install: handlers declare systemDeps, registry auto-installs with auto_install: true
- Validate mode: dry-run before deployment, integrated in rebuild.sh
- Handler auto-init for zero-config handlers
